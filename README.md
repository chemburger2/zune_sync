# Zune Web Manager

A browser-based management tool for the Microsoft Zune 30. The project is designed to communicate with the Zune directly through USB without requiring the original Zune software.

## Features

* USB Zune 30 detection and connection
* MTP/MTPZ communication and authentication work in progress
* Music management and transfer
* Album and track organization
* Picture management and transfer
* Video management and transfer
* Built-in FFmpeg/WebAssembly support for video conversion
* Transfer status and progress information
* Browser-based interface with no separate desktop application required

## Video Support

Video support is currently **experimental and incomplete**.

The manager can process and convert videos into formats intended for the Zune, but the resulting files do not yet consistently play correctly on a Zune 30. Some videos may transfer successfully but appear as missing, unsupported, or unable to play on the device.

Video compatibility and encoding are still being worked on, so video functionality should be considered a work in progress.

## Status

Music and picture management are further along, while direct Zune communication and video support are still under development. The project is experimental and may not work correctly with every Zune or media file.
